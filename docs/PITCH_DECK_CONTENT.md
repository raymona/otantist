# Otantist — Investor Pitch Deck Content

This document contains the full content for generating bilingual (EN/FR) pitch decks for potential investors and funding partners. Each slide has content in both languages plus speaker notes.

**Target audience:** Non-technical investors, funding bodies, social impact organizations
**Tone:** Warm, human-centered, professional but accessible. Avoid heavy jargon.
**Key message:** Otantist fills a critical gap — there is no social platform designed specifically for neurodivergent individuals. This is both a social good and a market opportunity.

## Slide 2: The Problem

**EN:**

- **Heading:** Social Platforms Weren't Built for Everyone
- **Points:**
  - 15–20% of the global population is neurodivergent (ADHD, autism, dyslexia, and more)
  - Existing social platforms create sensory overload — bright colors, animations, endless notifications, unpredictable interactions
  - Social norms are unwritten and enforced through exclusion — neurodivergent users often face misunderstanding, bullying, or isolation
  - Parents of neurodivergent minors have no visibility or tools to support safe online socialization
  - Moderation systems punish behavior without understanding context — a meltdown is not harassment

**FR:**

- **Heading:** Les plateformes sociales n'ont pas été conçues pour tout le monde
- **Points:**
  - 15 à 20 % de la population mondiale est neurodivergente (TDAH, autisme, dyslexie, et plus)
  - Les plateformes sociales existantes créent une surcharge sensorielle — couleurs vives, animations, notifications incessantes, interactions imprévisibles
  - Les normes sociales sont implicites et imposées par l'exclusion — les personnes neurodivergentes font souvent face à l'incompréhension, l'intimidation ou l'isolement
  - Les parents de mineurs neurodivergents n'ont aucune visibilité ni outil pour accompagner la socialisation en ligne de leur enfant
  - Les systèmes de modération punissent les comportements sans comprendre le contexte — une crise n'est pas du harcèlement

**Speaker notes:** This isn't about a niche audience. 1 in 5 people is neurodivergent. They're online, they want to connect, and every major platform fails them. The problem isn't that they can't use technology — it's that technology wasn't designed with them in mind.

## Slide 4: How It Works — Communication Adaptation

**EN:**

- **Heading:** Your Communication, Your Way
- **Features:**
  - "How to Talk to Me" guide — each user shares their preferences (preferred tone, communication modes, topics to avoid, personal tips) and others can read it before messaging
  - Preferred tone settings — direct, gentle, casual, or formal
  - Communication modes — text, voice messages, images — users choose what works for them
  - Conversation starters — topics they enjoy, topics to avoid, helpful tips for connecting

**FR:**

- **Heading:** Votre communication, à votre façon
- **Features:**
  - Guide « Comment me parler » — chaque utilisateur partage ses préférences (ton préféré, modes de communication, sujets à éviter, conseils personnels) et les autres peuvent le consulter avant d'écrire
  - Paramètres de ton — direct, doux, décontracté ou formel
  - Modes de communication — texte, messages vocaux, images — l'utilisateur choisit ce qui lui convient
  - Amorces de conversation — sujets appréciés, sujets à éviter, conseils pour créer un lien

**Speaker notes:** Imagine starting a conversation with someone and already knowing: "They prefer direct communication, they love talking about trains, avoid sarcasm, and they might take a while to reply — that's normal for them." That's what the "How to Talk to Me" guide provides. It removes the guesswork that makes social interaction exhausting for neurodivergent people.

## Slide 5: How It Works — Sensory & Energy Controls

**EN:**

- **Heading:** Respecting Your Senses and Your Energy
- **Features:**
  - Sensory preferences — reduce animations, lower color saturation, limit notification frequency
  - Social energy tracking — users set their current energy level so others understand their availability
  - Calm mode — one-tap quiet mode that queues all incoming messages, hides notifications, and suppresses typing indicators. Messages arrive when you're ready.
  - Time boundaries — set available hours per day. Messages sent outside your hours are queued and delivered when you're available.
  - Session focus timer — gentle reminders to take breaks (15/20/25/30 min presets)

**FR:**

- **Heading:** Respecter vos sens et votre énergie
- **Features:**
  - Préférences sensorielles — réduire les animations, baisser la saturation des couleurs, limiter la fréquence des notifications
  - Suivi de l'énergie sociale — les utilisateurs indiquent leur niveau d'énergie actuel pour que les autres comprennent leur disponibilité
  - Mode calme — un mode tranquille en un clic qui met en file d'attente tous les messages entrants, masque les notifications et supprime les indicateurs de frappe. Les messages arrivent quand vous êtes prêt.
  - Limites de temps — définissez vos heures de disponibilité par jour. Les messages envoyés en dehors de vos heures sont mis en file d'attente et livrés quand vous êtes disponible.
  - Minuterie de session — rappels doux pour prendre des pauses (options de 15/20/25/30 minutes)

**Speaker notes:** These aren't nice-to-have features. For many neurodivergent people, sensory overload and social exhaustion are daily realities. Calm mode alone changes the entire dynamic — you're never ambushed by notifications. You choose when to engage. Time boundaries mean a message at 11pm doesn't cause anxiety — it simply arrives at 9am the next day.

## Slide 6: How It Works — Safety & Moderation

**EN:**

- **Heading:** Safety That Understands, Not Just Enforces
- **Features:**
  - Human + AI moderation — AI flags potential issues, humans make the decisions
  - Gentle, clear communication — when moderation action is needed, users receive kind, plain-language messages explaining what happened
  - No surprise punishments — warnings are educational, not punitive
  - Content preservation — removed content is preserved for review, ensuring fair decisions
  - Blocking and reporting — simple tools, but with neurodivergent-friendly language and flow

**FR:**

- **Heading:** Une sécurité qui comprend, pas seulement qui punit
- **Features:**
  - Modération humaine + IA — l'IA signale les problèmes potentiels, les humains prennent les décisions
  - Communication douce et claire — quand une action de modération est nécessaire, les utilisateurs reçoivent des messages bienveillants en langage simple expliquant ce qui s'est passé
  - Pas de punitions surprises — les avertissements sont éducatifs, pas punitifs
  - Préservation du contenu — le contenu retiré est conservé pour examen, garantissant des décisions justes
  - Blocage et signalement — des outils simples, avec un langage et un parcours adaptés aux personnes neurodivergentes

**Speaker notes:** Most platforms moderate by punishing. A neurodivergent user who misreads social cues might get flagged, warned, or banned — without ever understanding why. Our moderation system explains things clearly, in language designed for people who may process communication differently. A warning message might say: "A message in this conversation was reviewed by our safety team. If you have questions, you can share your thoughts through the feedback form." Clear, non-threatening, actionable.

## Slide 7: How It Works — Parent Dashboard

**EN:**

- **Heading:** Empowering Parents, Protecting Minors
- **Features:**
  - Parent-managed accounts for minors (14–17) with secure linking codes
  - Activity indicators — parents see engagement patterns (not content) over the last 30 days
  - Real-time alerts — parents are notified when their child's content is flagged or moderation actions are taken
  - Minor protections — managed accounts are hidden from the general user directory and cannot be directly messaged by unknown users
  - Age group boundaries — minors cannot change their age group after setup, preventing circumvention

**FR:**

- **Heading:** Accompagner les parents, protéger les mineurs
- **Features:**
  - Comptes gérés par les parents pour les mineurs (14-17 ans) avec codes de liaison sécurisés
  - Indicateurs d'activité — les parents voient les tendances d'utilisation (pas le contenu) des 30 derniers jours
  - Alertes en temps réel — les parents sont avertis quand le contenu de leur enfant est signalé ou quand des actions de modération sont prises
  - Protections des mineurs — les comptes gérés sont cachés du répertoire général et ne peuvent pas être contactés directement par des utilisateurs inconnus
  - Limites d'âge — les mineurs ne peuvent pas changer leur groupe d'âge après la configuration, empêchant le contournement

**Speaker notes:** Parents of neurodivergent children face a difficult balance: they want their child to socialize online, but they worry about safety. Otantist gives them visibility without violating their child's privacy. They see patterns, not conversations. They get alerts when something needs attention. And the platform's design means their child is interacting in a safer environment from the start.

## Design Notes for Slide Generation

- **Color palette:** Calming, muted tones. Avoid bright/harsh colors (consistent with the platform's sensory-aware philosophy). Consider soft blues, greens, warm grays.
- **Typography:** Clean, readable sans-serif. Good spacing. Nothing cramped.
- **Visual style:** Minimal, spacious slides. More white space, fewer words per slide. Let the speaker fill in the details.
- **Icons/illustrations:** Simple, warm, human. Avoid corporate stock imagery. Abstract or illustrated people are better.
- **Accessibility:** The deck itself should be accessible — high contrast text, no information conveyed by color alone, readable font sizes.
- **Two separate files:** Generate `Otantist_Pitch_EN.pptx` and `Otantist_Pitch_FR.pptx` as independent decks (not one bilingual deck).
