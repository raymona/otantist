# Otantist — Funding Meeting Talking Points

_June 9, 2026_

---

## Opening — Why This Matters

- **1 in 5 people is neurodivergent.** That's 1.3 billion worldwide. Every major social platform fails them — sensory overload, unwritten social rules enforced through exclusion, moderation that punishes without understanding context.
- **There is no social platform designed for neurodivergent communication.** Not "added accessibility features on an existing app." Zero. Otantist is first-to-market.
- This isn't a niche — it's a massive underserved population at the intersection of mental health ($5.2B to $17.5B market by 2030), parental safety tools, and accessible tech.

---

## What We've Built (it's real, not a slide deck)

- **The platform is live and in beta testing right now** at otantist.app — invite-only, real users, collecting real feedback
- **48 features built** across these areas:

**Communication adaptation:**

- "How to Talk to Me" guides, preferred tone settings, conversation starters
- You know someone's communication style before you message them

**Sensory controls:**

- Reduce animations, lower saturation, control notification frequency
- The platform adapts to the user's nervous system

**Energy management:**

- Calm mode — one tap to queue all incoming messages
- Time boundaries — messages outside your hours arrive when you're ready
- Session timer with break reminders

**Safety & moderation:**

- Human + AI moderation (AI flags, humans decide)
- Gentle plain-language system messages
- Content preservation for fair review
- Enriched moderator tools — full conversation context, user profiles, reports
- A meltdown doesn't get treated like harassment

**Parent dashboard:**

- Activity indicators (patterns, not content)
- Real-time alerts on moderation actions
- Minor protections (hidden from directory, can't be cold-messaged)

**Technical foundation:**

- Fully bilingual French/English from day one
- WCAG 2.1 AA accessible — screen readers, keyboard navigation, semantic HTML
- Production infrastructure — Railway (API, DB, Redis), Vercel (web)
- Automated test suite (108 unit tests, 14 E2E tests), CI/CD pipeline

## Key Lines to Remember

- "We didn't add accessibility to an existing platform. We started with neurodivergent needs and built everything around them."
- "Otantist doesn't ask neurodivergent people to adapt to the platform. The platform adapts to them."
- "Imagine starting a conversation and already knowing: they prefer direct communication, they love trains, avoid sarcasm, and they might take a while to reply — that's normal for them."
- "Parents get visibility without violating privacy. They see patterns, not conversations."
- If asked about competition: "The closest alternatives are Facebook groups and support forums. There is no purpose-built social platform for this population. The market is empty."
